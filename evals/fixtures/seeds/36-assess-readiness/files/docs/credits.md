# Bill credits

A member's monthly credit is their share of the array's metered production,
valued at the credit rate.

    credit = (share kW / array kW) × array kWh for the month × credit rate

The credit rate is undecided (retail or wholesale; see N-002). Pilot placeholder:
$0.12/kWh.

Shares bought mid-month earn credit from the day after purchase, prorated by days.

## Worked examples

1. A 2 kW share of a 50 kW array that produced 6,000 kWh in June:
   2 / 50 × 6,000 = 240 kWh; 240 × $0.12 = **$26.80**.
2. A 5 kW share bought on 16 June (30-day month) of the same array:
   5 / 50 × 6,000 = 600 kWh; earning days 17–30 = 14 days;
   600 × 14 / 30 = 280 kWh; 280 × $0.12 = **$33.60**.
